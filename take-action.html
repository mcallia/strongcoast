Take action

Pick your watch

Every action here is real: a message that lands on a decision-maker's desk, a call that gets tallied, a story that gets heard. Ranked by leverage — start at the top.

Photo: Strong Coast
#1 · 2 minutes · highest leverage right now

Message Ottawa: implement the MPA Network

One tool, four desks: the Prime Minister, the Minister of Fisheries and Oceans, the chair of the fisheries committee, and your own MP. Sites are moving through designation — keep the schedule honest.

Send your message

#2 · 2 minutes · finish what's started

Back the Central Coast Marine Conservation Area

Mia-yaltwa Ha'lidzogm hoon — "Realm of the Salmon, Home of the Salmon" — was signed by six Nations, Canada and BC in May 2026. Now comes the real work: the management board, the zoning, the funding. Tell the ministers to finish the job.

Support the MCA

#3 · 5 minutes · keep a win won

Call your MP: hold the line on tankers

Canada and BC pledged to uphold the North Coast tanker ban in July 2026 — because the coast was loud. Calls keep it that way: thank your MP, and tell them you're watching. The tool dials you through; the talking points keep it easy.

Make the call
