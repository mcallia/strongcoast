Resources & allies

Good people, working for the coast

Strong Coast doesn't work alone. These are the organizations, community pages and publications we point people to — for local knowledge, science, stewardship, and the culture of this coast. We're not all the same, and we don't speak for each other; we share the water.

Coastal Nations & stewardship

Nations alliance

Coastal First Nations

An alliance of First Nations on BC's north and central coast and Haida Gwaii — Guardian Watchmen, marine stewardship, and the leadership behind the Great Bear Sea.

Science & stewardship

CCIRA

The Central Coast Indigenous Resource Alliance — restoring sustainable resource management for the Heiltsuk, Kitasoo Xai'xais, Nuxalk and Wuikinuxv Nations by weaving traditional knowledge with western science.

The plan

MPA Network — Northern Shelf

The First Nations–Canada–BC partnership planning and implementing the Great Bear Sea Marine Protected Area Network. The authoritative source on boundaries and zoning.

Fisheries & community

Community

West Coast Fishermen

The working fleet's own forum — a community page where BC fishermen swap news, conditions, and hard-won knowledge from the deck.

Salmon science

Pacific Salmon Foundation

Science and on-the-ground investment in wild Pacific salmon, working with communities and First Nations across the coast.

Culture & events

Strong Coast Guide

The festivals, fairs and gatherings that make this coast home — from the Comox Valley to Haida Gwaii to Smithers. The culture side of the Strong Coast family.

Science & education

Ocean education

The Marine Detective

Jackie Hildering's window into the cold, dark, life-rich waters of BC — the storytelling side of the Marine Education & Research Society.

Research & response

Marine Education & Research Society

Whale research, marine-wildlife response and ocean education out of Port McNeill — the science behind many of the humpback and orca stories on this coast.

Parks & ocean

CPAWS-BC

The BC chapter of the Canadian Parks and Wilderness Society — a long-standing voice for parks and marine protection in the province.

Law, policy & coast news

Environmental law

West Coast Environmental Law

Legal expertise and law reform for a healthy environment in BC — the people who help communities understand their rights on the water.

Coast news

West Coast NOW

North coast news and stories — part of the Strong Coast family of publications.

Regional journalism

The Skeena

Independent journalism from the Skeena region and BC's northwest.
