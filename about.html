About

Strong coast, strong communities

Strong Coast is an invitation to everyone who calls this coast home — and everyone who makes a living from it — to defend it from industrial damage and pass it on stronger than we found it.

We support the Great Bear Sea Marine Protected Area (MPA) Network through communication and education: plain-language explainers, stories from the people who work these waters, and tools that put your voice on the right desks in Ottawa and Victoria.

What we're not: Strong Coast is not affiliated with any political party, government, or industry group, and is not part of the Great Bear Sea MPA Network governing structure. We don't speak for First Nations — we amplify the leadership already happening on this coast.

The name? A coast is strong the way a rope is strong — many strands under load together: fishing families, First Nations, harbour towns, scientists, and everyone who eats from or answers to the sea. And strength is the standard: strong runs, strong stocks, strong communities.
